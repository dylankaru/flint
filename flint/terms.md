# Terminology used in **flint**
## Launchpad (not yet functional)
The portion of the launcher where the main action and search exists
## Pedestal (not yet built)
The portion of the launcher where the secondary/advanced controls and secondary options exist
## Radar (not yet built)
The portion of the launcher where the time-sensitive information and external extensions *can* exist\
- The radar isn't always visible, only when the information is meant to be displayed

### Exist
Be visible and interactable with/by the user
